Some of the most important emails require attachments, such as receipts, order confirmations, contracts, or digital assets. You need those emails to arrive automatically and reliably when customers and partners expect them. But attachments tend to increase bounce rates due to either security concerns, reduced engagement signals, or inbox provider policies, making deliverability harder to guarantee.
